How It Works

Simple, safe, and effective. Join our mission in three easy steps.

1. Donate

Restaurants or households post leftover food details with pickup time & location.

2. Pickup

Nearby volunteer drivers get alerts, accept jobs, and pick up the food.

3. Deliver

Food is delivered to shelters, homeless individuals, or workers in need.

Safety First Commitment

Every donation follows strict food safety guidelines.

  • ✔ Verified Donors
  • ✔ Temperature Checks
  • ✔ Contactless Handover

Donor Checklist

  • Food sealed properly
  • Date & time labelled
  • Allergens mentioned
  • Stored at safe temperature
